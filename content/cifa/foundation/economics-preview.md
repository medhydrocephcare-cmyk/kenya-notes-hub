## 1. Nature and scope of economics

**Economics** is the study of how individuals, firms, and societies allocate scarce resources among competing uses to satisfy unlimited wants.

### 1.1 Microeconomics vs macroeconomics

| Branch | Focus | Example question |
|---|---|---|
| Microeconomics | Individual decision-making units — consumers, firms, markets | Why does the price of a particular stock's underlying commodity rise? |
| Macroeconomics | The economy as a whole | Why is Kenya's inflation rate rising? |

### 1.2 Basic economic problem

Scarcity forces every economy to answer three questions: **what** to produce, **how** to produce it, and **for whom** to produce it.

### 1.3 Opportunity cost

The value of the next best alternative forgone when a choice is made. Central to economic reasoning: every decision, by an individual, firm, or government, has an opportunity cost.
