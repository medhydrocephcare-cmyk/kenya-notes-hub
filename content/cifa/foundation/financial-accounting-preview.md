## 1. The accounting equation and double entry

The accounting equation — **Assets = Liabilities + Capital** — must always balance. Every transaction is recorded using **double entry**: each transaction has a debit and an equal, corresponding credit. Debits increase assets and expenses (decrease liabilities, capital, income); credits increase liabilities, capital and income (decrease assets and expenses).
