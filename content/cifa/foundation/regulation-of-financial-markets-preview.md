## 1. Why financial markets are regulated

Financial markets are regulated to protect investors, maintain fair and orderly markets, reduce systemic risk, and build public confidence in the financial system. Without regulation, information asymmetries, conflicts of interest, and outright fraud could undermine trust to the point that investors withdraw from markets entirely.
