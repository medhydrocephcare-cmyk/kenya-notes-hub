## 1. Nature and importance of credit

**Credit** is an arrangement where a seller allows a buyer to take goods, services, or funds now and pay for them later, on agreed terms. Credit matters to a business because it:

- Widens the pool of customers who can afford to buy, growing sales volume.
- Builds customer loyalty and repeat business through an ongoing trading relationship.
- Is a normal feature of business-to-business trade, where most transactions are not paid in cash on the spot.

The flip side: credit ties up cash in debtors, and carries the risk that some customers will pay late or not at all. Credit management exists to balance these two forces — enough credit to win and keep customers, without so much risk that the business runs out of cash or absorbs unacceptable bad debts.
