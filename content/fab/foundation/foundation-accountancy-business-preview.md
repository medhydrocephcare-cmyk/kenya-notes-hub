## 1. Introduction to business

A **business** combines resources — land, labour, capital, and enterprise — to produce goods or services that satisfy customer needs, typically in pursuit of profit.

### 1.1 Forms of business ownership (brief)

| Form | Key feature |
|---|---|
| Sole proprietorship | One owner, unlimited liability, simplest to start |
| Partnership | Two or more owners sharing profit/loss per their agreement |
| Private limited company | Separate legal entity, liability limited to unpaid share capital |
| Co-operative society | Owned and controlled by members for mutual benefit |

### 1.2 Business functions

Most businesses perform core functions: production/operations, marketing, finance/accounting, and human resources.
