## Unit overview

**Unit description.** This paper extends Intermediate-level financial management into advanced corporate finance: complex investment decisions, optimal capital structure, dividend policy, mergers and acquisitions, and financial risk management through hedging and treasury techniques.

**Prerequisite.** A candidate attempting this paper is expected to have a secure grasp of Intermediate-level financial management and financial reporting.

**Learning outcomes.** A candidate who passes this paper should be able to evaluate complex investment decisions, estimate cost of capital, evaluate capital structure and dividend policy decisions, evaluate mergers and acquisitions, and apply risk-management/treasury techniques. *(Full learning outcomes list in the full version.)*

## 1. Investment appraisal under risk and uncertainty

Beyond straightforward NPV, advanced appraisal explicitly incorporates risk through sensitivity analysis, expected NPV using probability-weighted outcomes, and risk-adjusted discount rates for riskier projects.
