## Unit overview

**Unit description.** This paper builds the leadership and management judgement expected of a senior finance professional — moving beyond operational management into strategic leadership, organisational behaviour, and the judgement-heavy skills that distinguish a senior finance leader from a competent technician.

**Prerequisite.** A candidate attempting this paper is expected to have cleared CPA's Foundation and Intermediate levels, with a solid grounding in general management principles already established.

**Learning outcomes.** A candidate who passes this paper should be able to explain the role of strategic leadership, apply organisational behaviour concepts, evaluate approaches to organisational change, apply strategic HR concepts, evaluate ethical leadership dilemmas, and apply negotiation/conflict-resolution principles. *(Full learning outcomes list in the full version.)*

## 1. Strategic leadership versus operational management

**Operational management** focuses on efficiently running existing processes. **Strategic leadership** operates at a different altitude: setting long-term direction, shaping culture, and making judgement calls under genuine uncertainty, where a single "correct" technical answer often doesn't exist.
