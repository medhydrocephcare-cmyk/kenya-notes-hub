## 1. Activity-based costing (ABC)

Traditional absorption costing allocates overheads using a single volume-based basis, which can distort product costs when overheads are actually driven by non-volume activities (e.g. machine setups). ABC identifies distinct cost pools with genuine cost drivers, producing more accurate product costs.
