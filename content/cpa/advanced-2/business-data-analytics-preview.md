## 1. Data concepts and the data lifecycle

Data can be described at three levels: a **conceptual data model** describes, in business terms, what entities matter and how they relate; a **logical data model** adds structure without committing to a specific technology; a **physical data model** specifies exactly how data is stored in a given system. Data moves through a lifecycle: identifying sources, modelling requirements, obtaining, recording, using, and eventually removing data.
