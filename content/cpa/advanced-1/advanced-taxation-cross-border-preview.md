## 1. Residence and cross-border taxation

**Why residence matters:**
- Tax liability in Kenya turns on *residence*, not nationality.
- Residents: taxed on worldwide income (Kenya + foreign employment income), at graduated rates.
- Non-residents: taxed only on Kenya-sourced income, often at special/flat withholding rates.

**Individual — resident if:**
- Has a permanent home in Kenya and was present for any period in the year; **or**
- No permanent home, but present ≥183 days total in the year; **or**
- No permanent home, but present in the current year *and* averaged >122 days/year over the two preceding years.

**Body of persons — resident if:**
- Incorporated under Kenyan law; **or**
- Management and control exercised in Kenya during the year; **or**
- Gazetted as resident by the Cabinet Secretary, National Treasury.
