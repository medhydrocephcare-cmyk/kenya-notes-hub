## 1. Who is taxed: partnerships vs companies

Kenyan income tax treats these two common business vehicles very differently:

- A **partnership** is not a taxable "person" in its own right. Its profits are computed at partnership level, then allocated to and taxed on the individual partners, at their personal graduated tax rates.
- A **limited company** is a separate legal person and a separate taxable person. It pays corporation tax on its own profits, and shareholders are taxed separately (if at all) on what the company distributes to them.

This single distinction — taxable entity vs taxable-income pass-through — drives almost everything else in this chapter.
