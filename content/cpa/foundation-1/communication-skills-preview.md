## 1. The communication process and its purpose

**Communication** is the process of transmitting a message from a sender to a receiver, with the intention of it being understood as intended. The basic process: sender → encodes a message → transmits it through a chosen channel → receiver decodes the message → feedback returns to the sender confirming (or not) that the message was understood correctly. In business, communication serves several purposes: informing, instructing, persuading, motivating and building relationships between an organisation and its various stakeholders.
