## 1. Computer hardware and software

**Hardware** is the physical, tangible components of a computer (input devices like a keyboard/mouse, output devices like a monitor/printer, the CPU, and storage devices). **Software** is the intangible instructions directing hardware — **system software** (e.g. the operating system) and **application software** (programs for specific tasks, e.g. spreadsheets or accounting packages).
