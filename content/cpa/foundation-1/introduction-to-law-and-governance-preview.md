## 1. What is law, and the Kenyan legal system

**Law** is the body of rules, enforceable by the state, that governs conduct within a society. Kenya's legal system is organised around a court hierarchy — from subordinate courts, through the High Court, the Court of Appeal, up to the Supreme Court as the final appellate authority — with each level generally bound by the legal principles established by the courts above it.
