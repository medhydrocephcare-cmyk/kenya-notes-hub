## 1. Computer fundamentals — hardware

**Hardware** refers to the physical, tangible components of a computer system:

- **Input devices** — keyboard, mouse, scanner — used to enter data into the computer.
- **Output devices** — monitor, printer, speakers — used to present processed information to the user.
- **The central processing unit (CPU)** — often called the "brain" of the computer, it carries out instructions and performs calculations.
- **Storage devices** — hard disk drives, solid-state drives, flash drives — used to store data and programs, distinguished from the computer's temporary working memory (RAM).
