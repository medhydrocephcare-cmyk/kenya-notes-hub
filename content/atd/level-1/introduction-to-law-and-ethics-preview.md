## 1. What is law, and why does business need it?

**Law** is the body of rules, enforceable by the state, that governs conduct within a society. For business, law provides the predictable framework within which transactions, agreements and disputes can be conducted and resolved — without it, no trader could rely on a promise, a debt, or a contract being honoured or enforceable.

Business law specifically covers the rules affecting how businesses are formed, how they contract with others, how they employ people, and how disputes between them are resolved.
