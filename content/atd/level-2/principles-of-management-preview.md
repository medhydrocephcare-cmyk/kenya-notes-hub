## 1. What is management?

**Management** is the process of working with and through people and other resources (money, materials, information, time) to accomplish an organisation's goals efficiently and effectively. Two words matter here:

- **Efficient** — using the minimum necessary resources to achieve a given result (doing things right).
- **Effective** — actually achieving the intended goal (doing the right things).

A manager can be highly efficient (very economical) while still being ineffective (achieving the wrong outcome), and vice versa — good management aims for both together.
