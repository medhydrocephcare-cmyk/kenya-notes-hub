## 1. Recap: the accounting equation and double entry

The accounting equation — **Assets = Liabilities + Capital** — must always balance, and every transaction is recorded using **double entry**: each transaction has a debit entry and an equal, corresponding credit entry. Debits increase assets and expenses (and decrease liabilities, capital and income); credits increase liabilities, capital and income (and decrease assets and expenses).
