## 1. What is corporate governance?

**Corporate governance** is the system of rules, practices, and processes by which a company is directed and controlled — it defines how power is distributed and exercised among shareholders, the board of directors, management, and other stakeholders, and how the company is held accountable for its decisions. Good governance exists to protect the interests of shareholders and other stakeholders, reduce the risk of mismanagement or fraud, and build the trust that markets and society need to keep investing in and dealing with a company.
