## 1. The communication process and barriers to it

**Communication** is the process of transmitting a message from sender to receiver so it is understood as intended: sender encodes → transmits via a channel → receiver decodes → feedback confirms understanding. Common barriers: physical, language, perceptual/psychological, and organisational.
