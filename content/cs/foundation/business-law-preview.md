## 1. Nature and sources of law

**Law** is the body of rules, enforceable by the state, that governs conduct within a society. For a business, law defines what it may do, what it must do, and what remedies exist when rules are broken.

### 1.1 Classifications of law

| Classification | Distinction |
|---|---|
| Public law vs private law | Public law governs the relationship between the state and individuals; private law governs relationships between individuals/entities |
| Civil law vs criminal law | Civil law resolves disputes between parties; criminal law punishes conduct deemed harmful to society |

### 1.2 Sources of Kenyan law

- The Constitution — the supreme law.
- Legislation (statutes) enacted by Parliament.
- Subsidiary legislation made under an Act's authority.
- Case law (judicial precedent).
- Customary law, where applicable.
- International law and ratified treaties.
